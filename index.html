Our Story

A Legacy of Taste,
Warmth & Hospitality

Established in 1995, Panvel Palace has been the heart of Old Panvel's dining scene for three decades. From intimate family dinners to grand celebrations, we bring together the rich flavours of Mughlai, the freshness of Seafood, the boldness of North Indian cuisine, and the delicate artistry of Chinese cooking — all under one welcoming roof.

Our staff treats every guest like family. Whether you're here for a quiet lunch or a festive banquet — you'll feel it from the moment you walk in.
